Chapter 2: Domestic Flights

Domestic flights are the fastest and most comfortable way to cover long distances in Egypt. Multiple airlines operate domestic routes, with Cairo as the main hub connecting to Luxor, Aswan, Hurghada, Sharm El-Sheikh, Luxor and Alexandria.

Major Airlines

EgyptAir: national carrier, most routes, most reliable. Nile Air: budget option, good for Cairo–Luxor/Aswan. Air Cairo: budget carrier, domestic and regional routes. Air Arabia Egypt: budget option, good for Red Sea destinations.

Key Routes & Prices

Cairo–Luxor: 1 hour, $60–120. Cairo–Aswan: 1.5 hours, $70–130. Cairo–Hurghada: 1 hour, $50–100. Cairo–Sharm El-Sheikh: 1 hour, $60–120. Cairo–Alexandria: 1 hour, $50–90.

Booking Tips

Book early: prices increase closer to departure — book 4–8 weeks ahead for best prices. Compare airlines: EgyptAir is most reliable; budget carriers are cheaper but less flexible. Book directly: book through airline websites for best prices and flexibility.

Domestic Flight Tips

  • Arrive early: arrive at domestic airports 1.5–2 hours before departure for security.
  • Baggage limits: most domestic flights allow 23kg checked baggage + 8kg carry-on — check airline-specific limits.
  • Weather delays: sandstorms can cause delays in summer — build flexibility into your schedule.
  • Book return flights: book return flights at the same time as outbound for best prices and guaranteed seats.
  • Egypt Photography Tours: we book all domestic flights for multi-city tours, ensuring optimal connections and best prices.

Chapter 3: Sleeper & Day Trains

Egypt's train network connects major cities along the Nile Valley. Sleeper trains offer a unique overnight experience between Cairo and Luxor/Aswan, while day trains offer comfortable first-class service for shorter journeys.

Watania Sleeping Trains

Routes: Cairo–Luxor (10–12 hours), Cairo–Aswan (14–16 hours). Classes: First-class sleeper (private cabin, 2 beds), second-class sleeper (shared cabin, 4 beds). Price: $30–80 per berth depending on class. Experience: unique overnight journey through the Nile Valley — a memorable experience in itself.

Day Trains (First Class)

Routes: Cairo–Alexandria (2.5 hours), Cairo–Luxor (10 hours), Cairo–Aswan (14 hours). Class: first class offers air-conditioned, comfortable seats with more space. Price: $10–30 per ticket. Experience: comfortable day journey through the Nile Valley landscape.

Booking Tips

Book early: sleeper trains sell out quickly — book 2–4 weeks ahead, especially in peak season. Book at stations: tickets can be purchased at Ramses Station (Cairo) or through travel agents. First class: always book first class for comfort and air conditioning.

Train Travel Tips

  • Sleeper trains: bring water, snacks and entertainment — onboard food is basic. Bring a lock for your cabin.
  • First class: always book first class for air conditioning and comfort — second class is basic and crowded.
  • Arrive early: arrive at the station 30–45 minutes before departure for boarding.
  • Security: keep valuables secure and locked in your cabin on sleeper trains.
  • Egypt Photography Tours: we book sleeper trains for multi-city tours, ensuring first-class berths and optimal connections.

Chapter 4: Nile Cruises

Nile cruises are one of Egypt's most iconic travel experiences, offering a floating hotel experience between Luxor and Aswan with daily temple excursions. Multiple cruise options exist at different price points.

Standard Nile Cruises

Route: Luxor–Aswan (3–4 nights) or Aswan–Luxor (3–4 nights). Price: $50–150 per night. Includes: full board (all meals), daily temple excursions, onboard entertainment. Experience: comfortable floating hotel with daily guided excursions.

Luxury Dahabiyas

Route: Luxor–Aswan (3–5 nights) or custom routes. Price: $200–500+ per night. Includes: luxury accommodations, gourmet meals, private excursions, personalized service. Experience: intimate, luxury sailing experience on traditional wooden sailing boats.

Felucca Sailing

Route: short day sails or overnight sailing (1–2 nights). Price: $20–50 per day for day sails, $50–100 for overnight. Includes: traditional wooden sailing boat experience, meals on overnight trips. Experience: authentic traditional sailing experience on the Nile.

Nile Cruise Tips

  • Book early: Nile cruises sell out in peak season — book 2–3 months ahead for best availability.
  • Choose carefully: standard cruises are comfortable but basic; luxury dahabiyas offer a significantly better experience.
  • Check inclusions: verify what is included (meals, excursions, tips) — some cruises have hidden costs.
  • Check reviews: research cruise reviews carefully — quality varies significantly between cruise operators.
  • Egypt Photography Tours: we work only with verified, high-quality cruise operators and can arrange luxury dahabiya experiences.

Chapter 5: Uber, Careem & Taxis

For city transport in Cairo, Alexandria, Luxor and Aswan, Uber and Careem are the most reliable and convenient options. Traditional taxis are also available but require negotiation.

Uber

Availability: widely available in Cairo, Alexandria, Luxor and Aswan. Price: $3–15 per ride depending on distance and time. Advantages: transparent pricing, GPS tracking, cash or card payment, reliable service. Best for: most tourists — the most reliable and hassle-free option.

Careem

Availability: widely available in Cairo, Alexandria and other major cities. Price: similar to Uber, $3–15 per ride. Advantages: regional alternative to Uber, sometimes cheaper, reliable service. Best for: alternative to Uber, sometimes offers better prices.

Traditional Taxis

Availability: widely available everywhere. Price: $2–10 per ride depending on distance and negotiation. Advantages: widely available, can be cheaper than Uber. Best for: when Uber/Careem are unavailable — but always negotiate price before entering.

City Transport Tips

  • Use Uber/Careem: the most reliable, transparent and hassle-free option for city transport.
  • Download apps: download Uber and Careem apps before arrival and set up payment methods.
  • Negotiate taxis: if using traditional taxis, always agree on the price before entering the vehicle.
  • Avoid airport taxis: airport taxis are often overpriced — use Uber/Careem or pre-arranged transfers instead.
  • Egypt Photography Tours: we provide pre-arranged private transfers for all tours, eliminating transport hassles.

Chapter 6: Local Buses & Microbuses

For budget travelers, local buses and microbuses offer affordable intercity transport. These are basic but functional options for budget-conscious travelers.

Intercity Buses

Companies: Go Bus, Super Jet, Blue Bus. Routes: Cairo–Luxor, Cairo–Aswan, Cairo–Alexandria, Cairo–Hurghada. Price: $10–25 per ticket. Experience: comfortable air-conditioned buses for long-distance travel.

Microbuses

Routes: short intercity and suburban routes throughout Egypt. Price: $1–5 per ride. Experience: local transport experience — crowded but authentic. Best for: budget travelers and authentic local experiences.

Tips for Local Transport

Go Bus: most comfortable and reliable intercity bus company. Book ahead: book bus tickets a day or two ahead for guaranteed seats. Microbuses: pay the driver when you exit — exact change preferred. Safety: keep valuables secure on crowded microbuses.

Chapter 10: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best way to travel between Cairo and Luxor?
The best option depends on your priorities: Domestic flights (1 hour, $60-120) are fastest and most comfortable. Sleeper trains (10-12 hours, $30-80) offer a unique experience with sleeping berths and are significantly cheaper. Private transfers (10 hours, $150-250) offer door-to-door convenience with stops along the way. Egypt Photography Tours recommends flights for time efficiency and sleeper trains for the unique experience — both are included in our multi-city tours.
Is Uber available in Egypt and is it safe?
Yes, Uber is widely available in Cairo, Alexandria, Luxor and Aswan, and is generally safe and reliable. It is often cheaper than local taxis and eliminates negotiation hassles. Careem (the regional alternative) is also widely used. Both apps show the fare upfront, track your ride and allow cash or card payment. Egypt Photography Tours recommends Uber for most tourists — it is reliable, transparent and safer than hailing random taxis.
How do I book a Nile cruise in Egypt?
Nile cruises can be booked through tour operators (recommended), directly with cruise companies, or through hotels. Standard cruises run 3-4 nights between Luxor and Aswan. Prices range from $50-150 per night for standard cruises to $200-500+ for luxury dahabiyas. Egypt Photography Tours recommends booking through a reputable tour operator for guaranteed quality, verified safety standards and included excursions — all our multi-city tours include Nile cruise options.
What is the cheapest way to travel in Egypt?
The cheapest options are: Microbuses ($1-5 for intercity routes), local trains ($5-20 for second-class), and local buses ($3-10). These are significantly cheaper than private transfers or domestic flights but are less comfortable and less predictable. For tourists, Egypt Photography Tours recommends a balance: private transfers for long distances (comfort and reliability) combined with local transport experiences for short cultural segments.
Are Egyptian trains safe for tourists?
Yes, Egyptian trains are generally safe for tourists. First-class and sleeper trains are comfortable, air-conditioned and safe. Second-class trains are safe but more basic and crowded. Sleeper trains (Watania Sleeping Trains) are the most comfortable option for overnight journeys. Egypt Photography Tours recommends sleeper trains for the unique experience and first-class trains for day journeys — both are included in our multi-city tours.
How do I get from Cairo Airport to my hotel?
Best options from Cairo Airport: 1) Pre-arranged private transfer ($20-40) — most reliable and comfortable, driver meets you at arrivals. 2) Uber ($8-15) — reliable and transparent pricing. 3) Airport taxi ($15-25) — negotiate before entering. Egypt Photography Tours includes airport transfers in all our tours — your driver meets you at arrivals with a name sign, handles your luggage and drives you directly to your hotel.
