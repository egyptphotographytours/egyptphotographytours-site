Detailed Itinerary: 5-Day Nile Cruise

Day 1: Arrival in Luxor - East Bank Temples
Arrival in Luxor

Arrive at Luxor Airport or train station, meet your tour representative and transfer to your luxury Nile cruise ship. Complete check-in procedures and enjoy welcome drinks.

Karnak Temple Complex

Begin your exploration at the magnificent Karnak Temple Complex, the largest religious structure ever built. Walk through the Great Hypostyle Hall with its 134 massive columns and discover the sacred lake.

Luxor Temple

Visit Luxor Temple, beautifully illuminated in the evening. This temple was connected to Karnak by the Avenue of Sphinxes and was dedicated to the rejuvenation of kingship.

Evening on Board

Enjoy dinner on board and overnight stay in Luxor. Optional sound and light show at Karnak Temple available.

Day 2: Luxor West Bank - Valley of the Kings
Valley of the Kings

Cross to the West Bank of Luxor to explore the Valley of the Kings, where pharaohs of the New Kingdom were buried in elaborate tombs cut into the mountain. Visit three royal tombs (entry to Tutankhamun's tomb requires additional ticket).

Temple of Queen Hatshepsut

Visit the magnificent Temple of Queen Hatshepsut at Deir el-Bahari, dedicated to the first female pharaoh of Egypt. This stunning structure blends perfectly with the surrounding limestone cliffs.

Colossi of Memnon

See the massive Colossi of Memnon, two giant stone statues of Pharaoh Amenhotep III that have stood in the Theban necropolis for 3,400 years.

Afternoon Sail to Edfu

Return to the cruise ship for lunch as you begin sailing to Edfu. Enjoy the beautiful scenery of the Nile River and local life along the banks. Dinner and overnight on board.

Day 3: Edfu Temple & Kom Ombo Temple
Edfu Temple

After breakfast, visit the Temple of Edfu, dedicated to the falcon god Horus. This is one of the best-preserved cult temples in Egypt, with magnificent reliefs and a huge pylon.

Sail to Kom Ombo

Continue sailing to Kom Ombo while enjoying lunch on board. Watch the changing landscape and daily life along the Nile River.

Kom Ombo Temple

Visit the unique Temple of Kom Ombo, dedicated to both the crocodile god Sobek and the falcon god Haroeris. The temple's symmetrical design is unique in Egyptian architecture.

Crocodile Museum

Explore the adjacent Crocodile Museum which displays mummified crocodiles, once revered as manifestations of the god Sobek.

Evening Sail to Aswan

Continue sailing to Aswan. Enjoy dinner and Egyptian entertainment on board. Overnight in Aswan.

Day 4: Aswan - High Dam & Philae Temple
Aswan High Dam

Visit the Aswan High Dam, an engineering marvel completed in 1970 that controls the Nile's flooding and provides electricity and water for irrigation.

Unfinished Obelisk

See the Unfinished Obelisk in the ancient granite quarries, which would have been the largest obelisk ever erected if completed.

Philae Temple

Take a motorboat to Agilkia Island to visit the beautiful Philae Temple, dedicated to the goddess Isis. This temple was rescued from the rising waters of Lake Nasser and meticulously relocated.

Optional Felucca Ride

Afternoon at leisure. Optional traditional felucca sailboat ride around the islands of Aswan with views of the Aga Khan Mausoleum and Elephantine Island.

Farewell Dinner

Enjoy a farewell dinner on board with Egyptian themed night. Overnight in Aswan.

Day 5: Disembarkation & Departure
Breakfast & Check-out

Enjoy your final breakfast on board before disembarkation. Complete check-out procedures.

Optional Abu Simbel Tour

Optional morning tour to Abu Simbel temples (additional cost), or free time for last-minute souvenir shopping at Aswan's colorful markets.

Transfer to Airport

Transfer to Aswan Airport or train station for your departure, marking the end of your unforgettable Nile cruise journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the 5-Day Nile Cruise?
The cruise includes 4 nights accommodation on a 5-star luxury Nile cruise ship, all meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner), all entrance fees to the mentioned sites and temples, an expert Egyptologist guide throughout, all transfers in modern air-conditioned vehicles, all shore excursions, bottled water during tours, and all service charges and taxes. Not included: international airfare, Egypt entry visa, personal expenses, gratuities, travel insurance, and optional activities.
Which temples and sites will I visit on the cruise?
You will visit Karnak Temple Complex and Luxor Temple in Luxor, the Valley of the Kings, Temple of Queen Hatshepsut and the Colossi of Memnon on the West Bank, Edfu Temple dedicated to Horus, the double temple of Kom Ombo with its Crocodile Museum, Philae Temple on Agilkia Island, the Unfinished Obelisk, and the Aswan High Dam. An optional Abu Simbel excursion is available on the final morning.
Are meals and drinks included on the Nile cruise?
Yes — all meals are included during the cruise: breakfast, lunch, and dinner served on board, prepared with fresh local ingredients. Welcome drinks are served on embarkation day, and bottled water is provided during all shore excursions. Special dietary requirements (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, halal) can be accommodated — just mention them in the special requests field when booking.
What is the cruise ship like? Is it really 5-star?
Yes — we use premium 5-star Nile cruise vessels with comfortable en-suite cabins (air conditioning, private bathroom, large windows or balcony), a sun deck with swimming pool and loungers, an elegant restaurant, a lounge bar, and evening entertainment. Cabins are serviced daily. If you have specific cabin preferences (upper deck, twin beds, etc.), note them at booking and we will do our best to accommodate.
Can I add Abu Simbel to my Nile cruise?
Absolutely. Abu Simbel is offered as an optional early-morning excursion on Day 5 before your departure (additional cost, by road or short flight). Many guests also extend their trip with our other tours after the cruise. Contact us via WhatsApp (+20-155-073-5673) and we will arrange the perfect combination for your dates.
What is the cancellation policy for the Nile cruise?
Free cancellation is available up to 7 days before your cruise start date. Cancellations between 7 and 3 days before departure incur a 50% charge, and cancellations within 72 hours are non-refundable, as cabins are reserved and meals pre-arranged with the vessel. Date changes are possible subject to availability. Travel insurance is strongly recommended for all bookings.
