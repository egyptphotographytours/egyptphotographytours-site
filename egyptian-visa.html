Chapter 2: The e-Visa Process, Step by Step

Official e-Visa Application Guide

Step Action Time Required Critical Tip
1 Visit the official portal: visa2egypt.gov.eg 2 minutes NEVER use third-party resellers charging $80-150 for the same visa
2 Create account and start a new tourist application 5 minutes Use an email you check daily—your visa arrives there
3 Enter passport details EXACTLY as printed 10 minutes One wrong character can void the visa at the border
4 Upload passport bio-page scan 5 minutes Passport must be valid 6+ months from entry date
5 Pay $25 (single) or $60 (multiple) by card 3 minutes Some banks block foreign transactions—have a backup card
6 Receive e-Visa by email, print 2 copies 1-7 days Carry printed copies—phone-only is risky if battery dies

e-Visa Scam Alert

Search results are flooded with unofficial "Egypt e-Visa" sites charging 3-5x the real fee and harvesting passport data. The only official portal is visa2egypt.gov.eg. If a site charges more than $25/$60, asks for unusual personal data, or has a generic design—it's a reseller at best, a scam at worst. When you book any tour with us, we send you the direct official link and walk you through every field.

Chapter 3: Visa on Arrival, Step by Step

Airport Visa Process (Cairo Example)

  • Step 1 — Before passport control: Look for the bank kiosk (CIB/NBE) marked "Visa." Buy the $25 USD sticker. Carry exact cash—kiosks sometimes can't break large bills or accept cards.
  • Step 2 — Stick it in your passport: The teller hands you a physical visa sticker. Place it on a blank passport page.
  • Step 3 — Passport control: Join the regular immigration line. Officer stamps you in. Total time: 10-20 minutes off-peak.
  • Step 4 — Exit & meet your guide: Egypt Photography Tours clients with meet-and-greet are met inside the terminal—look for our name sign after baggage claim.

e-Visa vs. Visa on Arrival: Which Should You Choose?

Choose e-Visa if: You want guaranteed entry, you're arriving during peak hours, or you simply hate uncertainty. It's worth the 5 minutes of online form-filling.
Choose visa on arrival if: Your nationality is eligible, you have $25 USD cash ready, and you're comfortable with a brief kiosk stop. It's genuinely smooth at Cairo.
Our honest advice: For first-time visitors, get the e-Visa. For repeat visitors with USD cash, arrival visas are perfectly fine. Either way, we verify your eligibility before you fly.

Frequently Asked Questions About Egyptian Visas

Do I need a visa to enter Egypt?
Most nationalities need a visa to enter Egypt, including citizens of the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and EU countries. A few Gulf Cooperation Council nationals are exempt. Travelers can typically choose between an e-Visa (applied online before travel) or a visa on arrival at Cairo, Luxor, Aswan, and other international airports. Always verify your specific nationality's requirements 4-6 weeks before travel. Egypt Photography Tours provides free visa guidance to all tour clients as part of our pre-trip support.
How do I apply for an Egyptian e-Visa?
Apply through the official Egyptian government portal (visa2egypt.gov.eg) — never through third-party resellers charging inflated fees. Steps: 1) Create an account, 2) Fill in passport and travel details exactly as they appear in your passport, 3) Upload a passport scan, 4) Pay the $25 (single entry) or $60 (multiple entry) fee by card, 5) Receive your e-Visa by email within 1-7 days. Print it and carry a copy. Apply at least 7 days before departure. Egypt Photography Tours can walk you through the process step by step if you book a tour.
Can I get a visa on arrival in Egypt?
Yes — citizens of most Western countries (US, UK, EU, Canada, Australia, Japan, South Korea) can obtain a visa on arrival at Cairo, Luxor, Aswan, Hurghada, and Sharm El Sheikh airports. Buy the $25 USD visa sticker at the bank kiosk BEFORE passport control, then present it with your passport. Carry exact USD cash — cards are sometimes not accepted at the kiosk. Visa on arrival is single-entry and valid for 30 days. For smoother entry, we still recommend the e-Visa when available.
How much does an Egyptian visa cost in 2026?
Standard 2026 fees: Single-entry visa (e-Visa or on arrival): $25 USD. Multiple-entry e-Visa: $60 USD. Fees are payable in USD cash at airport kiosks or by card online. Beware of unofficial websites charging $80-150 for the same visa — only use visa2egypt.gov.eg for e-Visas. Prices are the same regardless of nationality for eligible countries. Egypt Photography Tours never charges a markup for visa guidance — it's included free with every tour booking.
How long is an Egyptian tourist visa valid?
Both the single-entry visa on arrival and the single-entry e-Visa allow a stay of up to 30 days from entry. The multiple-entry e-Visa is valid for 90 days from issue with up to 30 days per entry. If your trip exceeds 30 days, you can extend at the Mogamma immigration office in Cairo (allow a full day) or apply for the multiple-entry option. Most Egypt Photography Tours itineraries fit comfortably within the 30-day window.
