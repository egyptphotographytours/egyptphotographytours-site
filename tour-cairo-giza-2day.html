Detailed 2-Day Cairo & Giza Itinerary

Day 1: Giza Pyramids & Grand Egyptian Museum

8:00 AM - Hotel Pickup: Your private air-conditioned vehicle and certified Egyptologist guide will pick you up from your Cairo or Giza hotel.

8:30 AM - Giza Plateau Exploration: Begin your journey at the Giza Pyramids Complex, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Your Egyptologist guide will provide fascinating insights into:

  • Great Pyramid of Khufu (Cheops): The largest pyramid and one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World. Learn about its construction, purpose, and the mysteries that still surround it.
  • Pyramid of Khafre: Notice the remaining limestone casing at the top and the Sphinx Temple nearby.
  • Pyramid of Menkaure: The smallest of the three main pyramids, showcasing the evolution of pyramid construction.
  • The Great Sphinx: This enigmatic limestone statue with the body of a lion and head of a pharaoh has guarded the plateau for over 4,500 years.
  • Valley Temple: Explore this remarkably preserved temple where mummification rituals took place.

Professional Photoshoot Session #1: Your photographer will capture stunning portraits and landscapes at the panoramic viewpoint with all three pyramids in the background - the iconic shot you've dreamed of!

12:30 PM - Lunch: Enjoy authentic Egyptian cuisine at a local restaurant with pyramid views (lunch included in premium packages).

2:30 PM - Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM): Visit the world's most anticipated museum, housing over 100,000 artifacts spanning 5,000 years of Egyptian civilization. Highlights include:

  • The Complete Tutankhamun Collection: All 5,000+ treasures from the boy king's tomb displayed together for the first time.
  • The Grand Staircase: Massive statues of kings and gods welcoming you to the museum.
  • The King's Gallery: Royal mummies and artifacts from Egypt's greatest pharaohs.
  • Interactive Exhibits: State-of-the-art displays bringing ancient Egypt to life.

5:30 PM - Papyrus Institute (Optional): Learn about ancient Egyptian paper-making techniques and see demonstrations of papyrus art creation.

7:00 PM - Hotel Drop-off: Return to your hotel after an unforgettable first day exploring ancient Egypt's greatest treasures.

Day 2: Islamic Cairo, Coptic Cairo & Khan El Khalili

9:00 AM - Hotel Pickup: Your guide and driver will pick you up to begin your cultural journey through Cairo's historic districts.

9:45 AM - Citadel of Salah El-Din: Explore this 12th-century Islamic-era citadel offering panoramic views of Cairo. Visit:

  • Mohamed Ali Mosque (Alabaster Mosque): This stunning Ottoman-style mosque dominates the Cairo skyline with its elegant minarets and alabaster walls.
  • National Military Museum: Learn about Egypt's military history from ancient times to the present.
  • Panoramic Viewpoint: Capture breathtaking photos of Cairo's sprawling cityscape.

Professional Photoshoot Session #2: Capture the architectural beauty of Islamic Cairo and stunning city views from the Citadel.

12:00 PM - National Museum of Egyptian Civilization (NMEC): Visit this modern museum showcasing Egyptian civilization from prehistoric times to the present day. The highlight is the Royal Mummies Hall, where 22 royal mummies including Ramses II, Hatshepsut, and Seti I are displayed in a climate-controlled, respectfully presented environment.

1:30 PM - Lunch: Traditional Egyptian lunch at a local restaurant.

3:00 PM - Coptic Cairo: Step back in time in this ancient Christian quarter, one of the oldest parts of Cairo. Visit:

  • The Hanging Church (Al-Muallaqa): Built atop a Roman fortress gatehouse, this 3rd-century church is one of the oldest in Egypt.
  • Church of St. Sergius and Bacchus (Abu Serga): Built over the cave where the Holy Family (Jesus, Mary, and Joseph) is believed to have rested during their flight to Egypt.
  • Ben Ezra Synagogue: Egypt's oldest synagogue, dating back to the 9th century.
  • Coptic Museum: Explore the world's largest collection of Coptic Christian artifacts (optional visit).

4:30 PM - Khan El Khalili Bazaar: Immerse yourself in the vibrant atmosphere of Cairo's most famous marketplace, operating since the 14th century. Wander through narrow alleyways filled with:

  • Traditional crafts and souvenirs
  • Spice merchants with aromatic displays
  • Gold and silver jewelry shops
  • Perfume and essential oil vendors
  • Traditional coffee shops (try Egyptian tea or coffee)

Professional Photoshoot Session #3: Capture the colors, textures, and energy of this historic bazaar - perfect for cultural and street photography.

6:00 PM - Final Drop-off: Return to your hotel with memories of a complete Cairo experience spanning ancient pharaonic, Christian, and Islamic heritage.

🎯 Customization Available: This is a private tour, meaning you have complete flexibility. Want to spend more time at the pyramids? Add the Egyptian Museum in Tahrir Square? Skip a site? Just let us know - we'll customize the itinerary to match your interests and pace!
